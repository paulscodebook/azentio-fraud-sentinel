# Abnormal Amount Ratio

## Category
Behavioral Anomaly

## Definition
Transaction amount exceeds the customer account historical average by 3.0x or greater.

## Risk Rationale
Sudden volume spikes relative to historic baselines are primary indicators of account takeover (ATO) or card skimming.
