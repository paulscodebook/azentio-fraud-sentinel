# Unrecognized New Device

## Category
Authentication Risk

## Definition
Transaction initiated from a hardware device ID not previously linked to the account.

## Risk Rationale
New hardware fingerprints during non-routine purchases correlate strongly with malware session hijacking and credential theft.
