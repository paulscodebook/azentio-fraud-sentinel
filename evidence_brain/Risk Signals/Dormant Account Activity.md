# Dormant Account Activity

## Category
Account Lifecycle Risk

## Definition
Financial activity initiated on an account flagged as DORMANT, INACTIVE, or CLOSED.

## Risk Rationale
Dormant accounts are prime targets for insider fraud and account takeover because genuine holders rarely monitor statements.
